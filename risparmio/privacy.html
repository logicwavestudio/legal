Privacy Policy — Risparmio

Effective date: 8 June 2026 · Last updated: 8 June 2026

This Policy explains how the mobile app Risparmio (the “App”), developed by LogicWave Studio (“we”), handles your information. Contact: logicwavestudio@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • Your data (transactions, amounts, budgets, goals, documents) is stored locally on your device. We run no servers and we do not receive or collect your data.
  • No ads, no analytics/tracking, no in-app purchases.
  • Receipt/invoice photos are processed on the device (text recognition) and are not uploaded.
  • Optional features: Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup. If you enable them, data is also handled under Google’s privacy policy.

2. Data we process

2.1 Camera & photos

The App accesses camera/gallery only to capture receipts and invoices. Images are processed locally via Google ML Kit (on-device text recognition) and saved on your device as PDFs in the archive. No image is uploaded or transmitted by us.

2.2 Financial data (local)

Transactions, amounts, categories, budgets, goals, notes and document metadata are stored locally in a database on your device. They never leave the device unless you enable Google Drive backup (§2.4).

2.3 Google account (optional)

If you sign in with Google, the App obtains your account name, email and profile picture, used to show your profile and enable backup. This stays on your device (and in your backup, if any) and is not sent to us.

2.4 Google Drive backup (optional)

If you enable backup, a copy of your data (transactions, budgets, goals, profile) is stored in the private app folder of your Google Drive (drive.appdata scope), accessible only to this App. We have no access to it. Document PDFs stay on the device and are not part of the backup.

2.5 Permissions

  • Camera: to scan receipts/invoices.
  • Internet: only for Google Sign-In and Drive backup (if you use them).

3. How we use data

Data is used solely to run the App on your device: record and display expenses, statistics, budgets and goals, and — if you choose — sync a backup to your own Drive. We do not profile users and do not use data for advertising.

4. Data sharing

We do not share your data with third parties. The only third parties involved are the Google services (Google Sign-In, Google Drive) you choose to enable, governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Retention & deletion

  • Local data remains while the App is installed. Uninstalling the App removes local data and PDFs from the device.
  • To remove the Drive backup: revoke the App’s access from your Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions) and/or delete the app data from Google Drive.
  • For help or deletion requests, email logicwavestudio@gmail.com.

6. Security

Data is stored on your device. Communication with Google services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We may update this Policy; the “last updated” date above indicates the current version.
